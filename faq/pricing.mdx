You pay for **throughput, not seats**. Viewers are unlimited on every
plan — PMs, stakeholders, and eng leads see the board for free. What
you pay for is **sync events** (the updates devs and agents push to
the board) and, on paid plans, the number of active pushers.

<Note>
  Free is available today. Solo, Pro, and Scale are listed below
  for transparency but are **not yet live** — they ship as the
  product moves out of early access. Until then everyone is on the
  Free tier. See [`no-tickets.com#pricing`](https://no-tickets.com#pricing)
  for the current canonical pricing.
</Note>

## What counts as a sync event?

Every update a dev or agent pushes to the board counts as one sync
event — feature status changes, task completions, test-count
updates, progress recalculations. **Reading the board is free.**

A typical TDD cycle (red → green → refactor) emits 3-5 sync events.
A single feature with ~10 tasks across two days of work usually
sits in the 50-100 sync event range.
